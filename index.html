What types of facilities does M30 Performance manage?

M30 Performance provides full-service fitness management for HOA communities, corporate facilities, resorts, hotels, apartment communities, retirement communities, and existing fitness centers. Programs can be customized based on the facility, population, goals, and services needed.

How much does fitness management with M30 Performance cost?

Every facility is different, so M30 Performance does not use a one-size-fits-all pricing model. Contract pricing is customized based on factors such as facility size, number of residents or employees, location, staffing needs, programming, and services provided. Our low-overhead business model allows us to offer highly competitive pricing and significant value.

What makes M30 Performance different from other fitness management companies?

M30 Performance combines 30+ years of fitness industry experience, professional management, proven results, personal training, group exercise, wellness programming, member engagement, and equipment oversight into one comprehensive solution. Our goal isn't simply to manage your fitness center — it's to build a healthier, more active community while providing exceptional service and value.
